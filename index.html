About
CHARISH JOY

CHARISH JOY is an independent artist creating intimate cinematic story-pop: songs built around one clear feeling, one human story, and the lines we wish we had said out loud.

Her growing catalog moves across English, Korean and Tagalog while keeping the same heart — emotionally direct songwriting and warm, cinematic storytelling.
