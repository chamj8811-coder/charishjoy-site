Independent Artist | Music & Storytelling

CHARISH JOY is an independent artist creating original music across different sounds and emotions.

Exploring pop, K-pop, R&B, and heartfelt ballads, her music captures stories of love, longing, hope, change, and the moments that shape our lives.

From intimate ballads to energetic pop, each song offers a different sound while keeping storytelling at the heart of her artistry.

Creating music in English and Korean, CHARISH JOY continues to explore new musical directions without being defined by a single genre.
